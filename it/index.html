Three CIO forecasts for every company (13) · base session Monday 16 Nov 2026

Each panel shows that CIO's own recorded forecast for 5, 10 and 20 trading days (T+5, T+10, T+20): the direction (UP, DOWN or ABSTAIN), the recorded probability, the CIO's confidence, its evidence status and its recorded explanation. F&O eligibility basis: NSE F&O list of 13 Nov 2026 (the information-cutoff session of this base). ▲ UP / ▼ DOWN with the CIO's recorded probability of the stated direction; ABSTAIN = the CIO recorded no direction (its recorded reason is shown). Confidence (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH) is the CIO's view of its evidence, separate from the probability. PRICE-ONLY = no company research evidence was held in that CIO's pack, so the forecast rests on price data only (CIO-B always uses price data only).
