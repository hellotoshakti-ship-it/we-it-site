How it works. Before the 09:15 IST open, CIO-A (a research analyst), CIO-B (a price-pattern model) and CIO-C (research combined with CIO-B's model) each record, for every stock and horizon, a direction and the probability that the price will be higher than at the session's open.

Each forecast is frozen in a hash-chained ledger before the market opens and is never edited afterwards; the ledger is pushed to GitHub as an external timestamp.

A call is scored when its window has fully traded: the split- and bonus-adjusted close of the last day of the window against the open of the forecast session (dividends are not added back).

Probabilities are the CIOs' own recorded numbers, shown as recorded (rounded to whole percent) and never combined. Reasons are the CIO's recorded words with abbreviations spelled out and internal reference codes removed.

Experimental research. Not investment advice. Not a guarantee of returns. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell.
