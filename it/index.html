F&O eligibility basis: NSE F&O list of 6 Nov 2026 (the information-cutoff session of this base). ▲ UP / ▼ DOWN with the CIO's recorded probability of the stated direction; ABSTAIN = the CIO recorded no direction (its recorded reason is shown). Confidence (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH) is the CIO's view of its evidence, separate from the probability. PRICE-ONLY = no company research evidence was held in that CIO's pack, so the forecast rests on price data only (CIO-B always uses price data only).

A call is scored when its window has fully traded: the split- and bonus-adjusted close of the last day of the window against the open of the base session (dividends are not added back).

Probabilities are the CIOs' own recorded numbers, shown as recorded (rounded to whole percent) and never combined. Reasons are the CIO's recorded words with abbreviations spelled out and evidence codes removed (the forward institution's own six-digit record numbers are kept); a sentence that cannot be published as recorded is withheld and counted, never rewritten.
